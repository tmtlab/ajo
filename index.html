Groups

Staking and contributing both work by sending a token transfer to roscatesting with a memo (stake:<id> / contribute:<id>) — this contract has no separate "stake" or "contribute" action. The buttons below build that transfer for you with the exact amount the contract expects.

Fees & Incentives

System (Platform) Fee

The platform's own cut of every group cycle payout — separate from the organizer's fee and each group's own custom fee.

Personal Savings Fee

The fee charged on personal savings deposits (shown to the depositor before they confirm).

Panic Withdrawal Penalty

The penalty deducted when a saver withdraws from a personal savings plan before it matures (shown to the saver before they confirm a panic withdrawal).

Minimum Organizer Reputation

creategroup requires the organizer's reputation score to be at or above this. Brand-new accounts have no reputation record yet, so they'll need Early Reputation (below) before they can organize a group at all.

Minimum Reputation Bond

The minimum amount a "reputation" memo transfer must carry to initialize full (non-early) reputation.

Organizer Incentives

When disabled, creategroup rejects every new group (existing groups are unaffected) — this is the master switch creategroup checks before anything else.
